Icons

One open-source set, one stroke style, 24 px grid. Each icon moves once when its control is hovered or focused; nothing loops except a loader. Sources and licences: icons/SOURCES.md.

{{ICON:search:scan}}

Sizes

16 in dense tables · 20 in buttons and menus · 24 alone · 28–32 in empty states next to a title.

Colour

Icons take the text colour (currentColor). Status icons take their status colour, always with a label.
