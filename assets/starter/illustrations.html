Usage

  • ▸
    Size. 160–240 px wide in an empty state, above a title, one sentence and one action. Under 120 px use an icon instead.
  • ▸
    Colour. The figure reads --hairline-*, mapped to the tokens: it follows the theme with no extra file.
  • ▸
    Text. Every figure has an aria-label saying what it shows; the read-out is decoration and is hidden from screen readers by the figure.
  • ▸
    Email. Emails cannot run scripts: use a PNG export of the figure at rest.
