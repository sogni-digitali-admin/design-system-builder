# Screen pack: booking / services

## Screens
1. **Service list** — duration, price, staff, availability hint.
2. **Booking flow** — service → staff (or "any") → date (calendar with available days) → time slots (grouped
   morning/afternoon, disabled with reason) → details (name, contact, notes) → confirmation; a summary panel all
   along; time zone shown when the visitor's differs.
3. **Confirmation** — add to calendar (ICS), reschedule and cancel links with the policy, map/address or video link.
4. **Back office calendar** — day/week views per staff, drag to move (with conflict warning), blocked times,
   no-show and late markers, colour per service type (chart palette), list view for phones.
5. **Customers** — history of bookings, notes, no-show count.

## Rules
Cancellation/reschedule policy visible before booking; slots refresh if taken meanwhile ("This time was just
booked — here are the nearest"); reminders by email/SMS 24 h and 2 h before; accessibility of the calendar grid
(arrow keys between days, slot buttons with full labels "Thursday 9 October, 14:30").

## Emails
Booking confirmed, reminder, rescheduled, cancelled, review request, staff notification.
